How these numbers are measured
  1. Readings come from the heat pump itself through Samsung SmartThings, with no extra meters: flow and return water temperatures, flow rate, compressor speed, outdoor temperature and refrigerant pressures, every 5 minutes.
  2. Electricity is the heat pump's own energy counter, which SmartThings reports every minute.
  3. Heat is worked out as flow rate × (flow − return temperature) × 4.18 ÷ 60. SmartThings rounds the flow rate down to whole litres, so half a litre a minute is added back.
  4. COP (coefficient of performance) is heat out ÷ electricity in, over the same stretch of time. A COP of 3 means 3 kWh of heat for every 1 kWh of electricity.
  5. Expected COP is a benchmark: 45% of the theoretical best (Carnot) for the temperatures the refrigerant is actually running at. A well-performing unit gets close to it.
Accuracy: the water temperatures come in whole degrees and the difference between them is often only 2 to 4°C, so individual readings can be well out. Daily figures average much of that away, but Samsung's own sensors appear to read heat low: a comparison with previous gas use suggests the real COP may be 25 to 30% higher than shown. A proper heat meter is being looked into, and the figures will be corrected if it shows a consistent difference.
