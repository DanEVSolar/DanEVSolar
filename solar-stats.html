How "My stats" works
  1. Click "My stats", then "+ Add my system" — enter your own panel/battery spec and monthly generation, home use, EV charging, export and grid import figures.
  2. Your data replaces the charts above with your own — Dan's published figures are never touched, edited, or overwritten by anything you enter.
  3. It's saved only in your browser (via localStorage), not sent to any server. Close the tab, come back later, and it'll still be there — but it won't follow you to a different browser or device.
  4. Switch back any time by clicking "Dan's stats" — your own entries stay saved in the background and reappear exactly as you left them when you click "My stats" again.
  5. Export / Import JSON lets you download your entries as a backup file, or move them to another browser or device — import it there and click Save to apply.
  6. 📊 Export to Excel downloads a nicely formatted spreadsheet (summary, monthly figures and system spec) of your own entered data.
  7. "Clear my data" permanently deletes your entries from this browser (after a confirmation) and switches you back to Dan's stats.
Note: clearing your browser's site data/cookies, or using private/incognito mode, will also remove anything you've entered here — export a backup first if you want to keep it.

Scenario assumptions
Where a month has an actual grid import figure entered, that's used directly instead of any estimate below — always more accurate.
Flexible, no solar: No battery. Home + EV consumption imported at the flat day rate.
Flexible, with solar+battery: Uses actual grid import if entered; otherwise estimated as the shortfall between self-consumption and an assumed typical daily demand (≈ battery capacity, or panel capacity × 3). Imported at the flat day rate — no cheap window on Flexible.
Int. Go, no solar, no battery: EV + 25% of home at off-peak; 75% of home at day rate.
Int. Go, with solar+battery: Uses actual grid import if entered, assumed off-peak. Otherwise modelled as battery charged fully overnight at off-peak, covering all daytime home load, plus EV at off-peak. Solar exported at full SEG rate.
